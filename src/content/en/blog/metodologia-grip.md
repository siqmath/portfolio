# GRIP Methodology: The Perfect Action

In systems engineering and *real estate*, theory often masks energy waste. 

The **GRIP** (Get, Remove, Implement, Prove) methodology acts as a surgical scalpel for product and ops management.

## 1. Get (Raw Diagnostics)
Capture raw data without filters. If you categorize immediately, you lose the nuances of systemic asymmetry (echoing Sapolsky).

## 2. Remove (Base Friction)
Before building architecture, eliminate useless architecture. Every project node that demands excessive human cognitive processing that could be delegated to a machine must be ripped out.

## 3. Implement (Traction Pull)
With a clean foundation, velocity is imposed. implementation isn't feature creep; it's securing the solution through bare essentials that scale linearly or exponentially.

## 4. Prove (Akita C-Level Method)
Code or processes that don't withstand practical pressure testing are theoretical delusions. Prove the ROI, prove the fluid package compile.

> "The bridge between a meticulous engineer and a ravenous executive is the brutal adoption of GRIP."
