Treat Artificial Intelligence like an extremely intelligent intern who has ten times more free time than you do. 

Many fail by trying to delegate the *thinking* itself. AI is not here to stop you from reasoning, nor to cure the laziness of starting and finishing a task. It exists to amplify what you have already decided to build.

### Rules of Engagement

1. **Clear and Determined Instructions**: A good intern needs excellent scoping. If the instruction is vague, the result will be mediocre.
2. **Retain Decision Power**: Never delegate to AI the weight of important decisions in your business or life. It processes data; you govern the direction.
3. **Feet on the Ground**: AI is not magic. It is an accelerator. It enhances your existing clarity and your ability to act upon organized logic.

### The New Agentic Era

We are entering a historic moment where, effectively, anyone can have digital "employees" working 24/7. This is the democratization of operational force.

But here is the bitter spoiler: **Not everyone is a good leader.**

If you don't know where you are going, leading a team of AI agents will only make you get nowhere faster. And worse: it will still cost you a good amount of money in tokens and infrastructure.

Leadership comes before execution. Direction comes before speed.
